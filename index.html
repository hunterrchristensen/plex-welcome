Install the app

Find your device and tap it. The app is free and it's just called Plex in every store.

Roku

Home, then Streaming Channels, then search Plex and add the channel. Open it and choose Sign In.

It shows you a short code. On your phone or laptop go to plex.tv/link, sign in, and type the code. The TV signs itself in.

Apple TV

App Store on the Apple TV, search Plex, hit Get.

Open it, pick Sign In, then enter the code it displays at plex.tv/link on your phone.

Fire TV / Fire Stick

Search Plex from the Fire TV home screen (the mic button works fine) and download it.

Open it, choose Sign In, and enter the code at plex.tv/link.

Android TV / Google TV / NVIDIA Shield

Google Play Store on the TV, search Plex, install it.

Open it, choose Sign In, then enter the code at plex.tv/link.

Samsung / LG / Vizio smart TV

Open your TV's app store (Samsung calls it Apps, LG calls it Content Store) and search Plex.

Sign in with the code at plex.tv/link.

Older TVs sometimes don't have Plex any more. If yours doesn't, a $30 Fire Stick or Roku is genuinely the easiest fix. You can also just cast from your phone.

iPhone / iPad

App Store, then Plex: Stream Movies & TV, then sign in with your own account. No code needed here, you just type your login.

You can AirPlay from here to most TVs if you'd rather not install anything on the TV itself.

Android phone / tablet

Google Play, then Plex: Stream Movies & TV, then sign in with your own account.

Chromecast works from here too.

Laptop or desktop (nothing to install)

Go to app.plex.tv and sign in. Works in any browser, and it's the fastest way to check everything's working.

Find my libraries

Sign in with your own Plex account, the one you made when you accepted the invite. Never mine. That's what keeps your watch history and your resume-where-you-left-off yours.

  1. 01

    Ignore Plex's own libraries

    The first thing you'll see is Plex's own free stuff: live TV, ad-supported movies, a wall of channels. None of that is mine and all of it has ads. You can ignore the lot of it.

  2. 02

    Look for rockethunter

    That's my server. It's in the sidebar on a computer, or under the menu on a TV or phone. My three libraries sit under that name. Nothing in them has ads.

    Each library has a pin icon next to it. Pin Movies, TV Shows and Kids TV and they'll sit right at the top of your sidebar every time you open Plex, so you never have to go hunting past the ad channels again.

Still stuck?

Nine times out of ten it's the first one. Force-close the app and reopen it before anything else.

It won't play, or it just spins

Close the app completely and reopen it. Then try a different show. If that one plays, the file's the problem and I'll replace it, so tell me which one.

If nothing plays, my server might be restarting. Give it ten minutes, then restart the app once more.

It keeps buffering

Restart the app first. If it's still stuttering, hit the settings icon while something's playing and pick a lower quality. It'll look slightly softer but it won't stop.

On hotel or mobile data, drop it right down. On good home wifi you can leave it on the highest setting.

I can't find the server, or I only see ads and live TV

You're looking at Plex's own free content, not mine. Open the sidebar or top menu and look for rockethunter. My three libraries sit under it.

If it isn't listed at all, you're probably signed into the wrong account, or the invite was never accepted. Sign out, sign back in as yourself, restart the app.

Subtitles aren't showing up

While it's playing, tap the screen and look for the speech-bubble icon. Pick an English track and it sticks for everything you watch after.

Some files genuinely don't have subtitles. Restart the app first in case it just didn't load them. If there's still nothing there, tell me and I'll sort that title out.

The audio's in the wrong language, or the voices are quiet

Same menu as subtitles: tap the screen while it's playing and look for the audio track selector, then pick the English one.

Quiet dialogue with loud explosions usually means a surround track playing through TV speakers. Switch to the stereo track if there is one. Restart the app if the menu looks empty.

Can I download things to watch on a plane?

On phones and tablets, yes. Look for the download arrow on a movie or episode, and do it on wifi before you leave.

If the arrow isn't there, restart the app. Still missing? Message me and I'll flip the setting for you.

If it's still broken

Tried the restart and it's still not working? Tell me what device you're on and what you were trying to watch. That's usually enough for me to fix it from here.
